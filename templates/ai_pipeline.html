End-to-End Intelligent Resolution Architecture

Every incoming support ticket passes through a 9-stage intelligent pipeline. Resolve IQ classifies the issue, assigns the appropriate department, retrieves relevant historical cases, assesses classifier confidence, prepares safe remediation guidance, detects recurring patterns, and applies human-review safeguards when required.

Lifecycle Stages

STAGE 01
Ticket Intake
Captures subject, description, requester information, priority, and available attachments.
STAGE 02
AI Classification
Predicts the support category using trained text vectorization and probabilistic classification.
Inspect Classification
STAGE 03
Smart Routing
Maps the predicted category to the appropriate support or engineering department.
STAGE 04
Knowledge Retrieval
Searches historical tickets and surfaces up to three relevant cases and their recorded resolutions.
Inspect Retrieval
STAGE 05
Resolution Synthesis
Generates a practical resolution suggestion using the most relevant historical ticket information.
STAGE 06
Confidence Scorer

STAGE 07
Safe Remediation
Prepares or performs supported non-destructive diagnostics while keeping sensitive operations within human-control boundaries.
STAGE 08
Recurring Detection
Identifies recurring issue patterns when multiple similar incidents appear in the ticket history.
STAGE 09
Escalation Safeguard
Applies confidence and safety boundaries to identify tickets requiring human specialist attention.
